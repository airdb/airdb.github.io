## Scope

This notice describes the public website at [airdb.com](https://airdb.com), operated by AIRDB PTE LTD ("Airdb"). It covers browsing our product information, blog and documentation, and contacting us about our services. It does not describe data processing inside a customer's separately deployed application or contracted service; those arrangements need their own terms and privacy information.

## Information you send us

The website's contact links open your email application. A message is sent only when you choose to send it. If you contact us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, any attachments and any other information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and discuss the services you request.

Please include only information needed for your enquiry. Do not send passwords, API keys, identity documents or sensitive customer data in an initial message.

This public website does not provide account registration, online payment or a newsletter sign-up form.

## Website delivery and local storage

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page or asset, the hosting provider receives technical request information, which may include your IP address, browser details and the requested URL. The provider's handling of that information is described in [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference and any article bookmarks you save. These preferences remain in that browser; the site's preference and bookmark features do not send them to Airdb. You can remove them by clearing site data in your browser. Clearing bookmarks removes your saved article list.

The public site does not include an analytics or advertising tracking integration.

## Email and external services

Email delivery involves your email provider and the provider handling our mailbox. Information sent in an enquiry is processed through those services to deliver and respond to the message.

Links to GitHub, social networks and other external sites take you to services governed by their own privacy notices. Using a social sharing link sends the selected page URL to the service you open. Review that service's notice before submitting information there.

## Privacy enquiries

To ask about information you have sent us, request access or correction, or request deletion or withdrawal of consent, email [support@airdb.cn](mailto:support@airdb.cn). Describe the relevant enquiry and the action you are requesting. We may need to confirm your identity before handling a request. Applicable requirements and any necessary business records may affect how a request can be fulfilled.

For questions about how long correspondence is retained, which providers handle it or where it is processed, contact the same address.

## Changes

We will update this notice when the public website's information handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
